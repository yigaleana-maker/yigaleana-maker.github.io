  1. DownloadTap the download button of the app you want, from your phone.

Your privacy

Headpod and WhyType need no account, have no ads and don’t collect your data. Your notes, shows and progress stay on your phone, and on your Google Drive only if you turn on backup. Read the full privacy notice.
