Privacy notice

Headpod and WhyType · Updated November 15, 2026

In short

The apps need no account, show no ads and don’t collect your data. Your stuff stays on your phone.

What is stored and where

  • Headpod: your shows, progress, lists and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • WhyType: your notes, photos, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. Private notes are protected with your fingerprint or your phone’s lock.
  • Backup: if you turn it on, a copy is saved to the folder you choose, for example your Google Drive. Nobody else sees it.

When they connect to the internet

  • Headpod connects to the shows you follow to fetch episodes, artwork and transcripts, and to Apple Podcasts when you search for a show.
  • WhyType only connects if you create an image with AI. Text scanning works offline.
  • Both check GitHub for new versions.

Crash reports

If an app closes because of an error, the next time you open it, it asks if you want to send a report. It is only sent if you send it yourself, from your email. It includes the error, the app version and your phone model, nothing else.

Permissions

Notifications (new episodes and reminders), camera (only to scan text in WhyType), Bluetooth (only to resume when you connect your headphones in Headpod) and installing updates. Each one is used only for that.
